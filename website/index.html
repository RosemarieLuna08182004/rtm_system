FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Roots & Morphology for?

Nursing, medical, chiropractic, physical-therapy and allied-health students — and anyone who wants anatomy terms to make sense instead of being memorised.

Do I need to know Latin or Greek?

Not at all. The app explains every root, prefix and suffix in plain English, with its origin, so you pick up the classical roots as you go.

Which phones does it work on?

Roots & Morphology is available for Android. The home-screen widget is Android-only.

Does it work offline?

You need an internet connection to load terms. If your connection drops, the app keeps your place and carries on when you’re back online.

Are my notes and bookmarks saved?

Bookmarks are saved to your account. Personal notes are saved on your phone.

Why are some volumes locked?

Some volumes and terms open only once they’re unlocked for your account. Unlocked content appears automatically.
